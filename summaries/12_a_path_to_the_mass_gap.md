# Chapter 12. A Path to the Mass Gap

This chapter assembles the results of the book into a programme for the mass gap of four dimensional SU(2) lattice Yang Mills. It lists five stages: positivity at every scale (reflection positivity, nonnegative character coefficients), strong coupling (cluster expansion), the Migdal Kadanoff renormalization ladder, the comparison of the true block spin flow on the lattice Z^4 with Migdal Kadanoff type flows (open, and the place where a new idea is needed), and the continuum theory. It explains why the bracketing arguments of Kadanoff and Tomboulis do not control spectral measures, poses the comparison as a Thorin sandwich between two Migdal Kadanoff type maps in the likelihood ratio order, and collects open problems: an explicit remainder in the decrement, the likelihood ratio property and monotonicity for SU(3), the next order of the decrement against the two loop beta function, a Bayesian gap estimator for glueball correlators, and the uniform Thorin gap inequality at strong coupling with explicit constants. It weighs three routes to the missing step, discusses recent work of others and unused ideas (centre vortices, monopoles, gradient flow, resurgence, holography, Hamiltonian lattice formulations, formal verification, loop equations, generalised symmetries), and argues for attempting three dimensional SU(2) first, where the flow is superrenormalizable, with the open problem of a mass gap of at least c_0 over beta at every coupling.

## From the literature

- Bond moving inequalities (Kadanoff 1975, 1976) and the Migdal recursion (Migdal 1975).
- Confinement on Migdal Kadanoff hierarchical lattices (Ito 1985).
- A claimed confinement proof by bracketing (Tomboulis 2007) and its critique (Ito and Seiler 2007, 2008).
- The strong coupling cluster expansion (Osterwalder and Seiler 1978) and the stochastic analysis approach (Shen, Zhu and Zhu 2023).
- Ultraviolet control by the renormalization group (Balaban 1987) and finite volume SU(2) Yang Mills (Magnen, Rivasseau and Seneor 1993).
- The three dimensional vacuum wavefunction and string tension (Karabali, Kim and Nair 1998) and lattice data (Teper 1999).

## New results

Open problems of the list are catalogued below, except the restated Thorin sandwich and the next order of the decrement, answered in Chapter 13.

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Thorin sandwich for the four dimensional flow](../RESULTS.md#thorin_sandwich) (Open problem 15, page 197; open): Find two Migdal Kadanoff type maps whose flows bracket the true spectral measures, with a decrement inequality for the lower one. New in this book.
- [Explicit remainder for the decrement of the Migdal Kadanoff ladder](../RESULTS.md#op_explicit_decrement_remainder) (Open problem 16, page 200; open): Bound explicitly the first correction to the leading decrement of the four dimensional SU(2) Migdal Kadanoff ladder, using the variance of the Biane, Pitman and Yor variable. New in this book.
- [Likelihood ratio property and monotonicity of the ladder for SU(3)](../RESULTS.md#op_likelihood_ratio_su3) (Open problem 17, page 200; open): Prove the monotone likelihood ratio in time of the heat kernel analytically and extend the monotonicity and covering arguments of the ladder to SU(3); the SU(2) half is solved in Chapter 13. New in this book.
- [Bayesian gap estimator for lattice glueball correlators](../RESULTS.md#op_glueball_gap_estimator) (Open problem 19, page 200; open): Apply the book's Bayesian estimator of the mass gap to published lattice glueball correlators and test the Migdal Kadanoff ladder against them at intermediate couplings. New in this book.
- [Uniform Thorin gap inequality at strong coupling with explicit constants](../RESULTS.md#op_strong_coupling_thorin_gap) (Open problem 20, page 200; open): Prove from the cluster expansion, with explicit constants, the uniform decay inequality for Thorin densities that is equivalent to a uniform spectral gap, and push it toward the crossover. New in this book.
- [Mass gap of order one over beta for three dimensional SU(2) by bracketing](../RESULTS.md#op_three_dimensional_gap) (Open problem 22, page 212; open): Prove a mass gap at least a constant over beta for three dimensional SU(2) lattice gauge theory at every coupling, by bracketing one block spin step between two contracting Migdal Kadanoff maps. New in this book.
