# Chapter 15. Neighbouring Problems

<!-- cite:begin -->
From *The Mass Gap: Thorin Measures, Brownian Motion and Yang Mills* by Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov (2026), chapter 15. Cite as Polson and Sokolov (2026), *The Mass Gap: Thorin Measures, Brownian Motion and Yang Mills*, edition 1, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24546905. This chapter collects problems next to the mass gap that share its mathematics. It begins with the Lee and Yang circle theorem, Polya's Hilfssatz II and Kac's equal coupling proof, puts them in Thorin form by a Frullani bridge, and applies the method to the smallest Yang Mills systems: one plaquette, whose reciprocal partition function is the Laplace transform of a Brownian hitting time of the sphere S^3 with Fisher zeros at Bessel zeros, and a two dimensional heat kernel torus whose partition function zeros lie on fixed rays. The second section reads stochastic quantisation, the lattice Langevin equation and nonlinear filtering (Zakai and Kushner Stratonovich equations) alongside the Migdal Kadanoff ladder, and proves a continuous scale form of the projected ladder with a beta function given by a covariance. The third reads the authors' Hilbert sixth problem book for Feynman Kac, Nelson's stochastic mechanics and Levy area derivations of the gap and of the coefficient 11/3. The last sections take from the Riemann and Langlands books the Sato Tate law as Haar measure on SU(2), Ramanujan graphs, passage times on representations, a latent count for the heat kernel, and a limitation theorem.

## New results

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Frullani bridge between Lee Yang zeros and Thorin form](../RESULTS.md#frullani_bridge) (Theorem 15.1, page 259; proved): For an even entire function of order one, real zeros are equivalent to Polya's operator preserving real zeros and to the reciprocal of its rotated form being the Laplace transform of a generalized gamma convolution. First stated in Polson (2026), From Polya's Hilfssatz II to the Thorin representation: a probabilistic synthesis, manuscript.
- [One plaquette partition function as a Brownian hitting time](../RESULTS.md#plaquette_hitting_time) (Proposition 15.2, page 260; proved): The reciprocal of the one plaquette SU(2) partition function in the squared coupling is the Laplace transform of the time Brownian motion in four dimensions takes to reach the unit sphere, a generalized gamma convolution with Thorin atoms at the Bessel zeros. New in this book.
- [Zeros of the heat kernel torus partition function on fixed rays](../RESULTS.md#zeros_on_fixed_rays) (Theorem 15.3, page 261; proved): For two dimensional SU(2) with the heat kernel action on a torus, the zeros in complex time lie on the same rays for every area, determined by the zeros of one theta type series, which has none in the disc of radius 0.83; the width of the zero free sector is only computed. New in this book.
- [Zero free sector for the Wilson action on tori and hierarchical lattices](../RESULTS.md#op_zero_free_sector_tori) (Open problem 25, page 262; open): Prove a zero free sector around the positive coupling axis, uniform in the volume, for two dimensional tori and for hierarchical lattices. New in this book.
- [The projected Migdal Kadanoff ladder in continuous scale](../RESULTS.md#projected_ladder_continuous_scale) (Proposition 15.4, page 267; proved): In continuous scale the Migdal Kadanoff ladder projected on the SU(2) heat kernel has a beta function given by a covariance, which is positive at every coupling. New in this book.
- [Why the ladder decrement reproduces the background field coefficient](../RESULTS.md#op_decrement_four_minus_third) (Open problem 26, page 270; open): Explain why the Migdal Kadanoff decrement built from the mean one third reproduces, at scale factor two, a coefficient that the background field computation writes as four minus one third. New in this book.
- [Passage times of the walk on SU(2) representations](../RESULTS.md#passage_times_on_representations) (Proposition 15.6, page 275; proved): The first passage time to the representation of dimension N for the continuous time walk on the representations of SU(2) is a generalized gamma convolution with explicit atoms and mean (N^2-1)/3. New in this book.
- [Latent count representation of the SU(2) heat kernel](../RESULTS.md#latent_count_heat_kernel) (Proposition 15.7, page 276; proved): The SU(2) heat kernel is a mixture of powers of one plus cosine with a sum of independent Bernoulli variables as exponent, so that given a latent count the half angle cosine squared is Beta distributed. New in this book.
