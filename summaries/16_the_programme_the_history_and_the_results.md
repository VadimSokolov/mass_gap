This closing chapter has three parts. First, a research programme of twelve numbered problems (with a problem 1a), ordered by estimated difficulty from certifying the Migdal Kadanoff ladder on hierarchical lattices, through string tension on hierarchical lattices, the likelihood ratio property for SU(3) and Griffiths type inequalities for SU(2), to the mass gap in three dimensions and the Clay Millennium problem in four. Second, historical notes on gauge theory from Weyl and Yang and Mills to Wilson's lattice formulation, constructive field theory from the Osterwalder Schrader axioms and Balaban's renormalization group work, hierarchical lattice confinement and the Tomboulis proposal, infinitely divisible laws and generalized gamma convolutions, and Monte Carlo and Markov random fields, together with a record of the 26 October 2026 announcement by OpenAI of an existence proof for four dimensional Yang Mills, endorsed by a review group chaired by David Gross but not refereed, disputed in public, and claiming neither confinement nor a mass gap; the authors have not checked it. Third, a summary of the results of the book with their status and home chapters, the computational findings, a scorecard of the ladder against lattice data, and the conjectures and open problems that remain.

## From the literature

Weyl's gauge principle (1918, 1929); the Yang Mills equations (Yang and Mills 1954); Faddeev and Popov quantisation (1967); renormalizability ('t Hooft 1971, 't Hooft and Veltman 1972); asymptotic freedom (Gross and Wilczek 1973, Politzer 1973); the Wilson lattice formulation (Wilson 1974); Creutz's SU(2) simulations (1980); the Osterwalder Schrader axioms (1973, 1975); Osterwalder and Seiler's reflection positivity and strong coupling gap (1978); Luscher's transfer matrix (1977); exact two dimensional Yang Mills (Migdal 1975, Gross, King and Sengupta 1989, Driver 1989, Witten 1991); Balaban's renormalization group analysis (1983 to 1989); Migdal Kadanoff recursions (1975, 1976) and Ito's confinement on hierarchical lattices (1985); Tomboulis's bracketing proposal (2007) and the Ito and Seiler critique; Thorin (1977) and Bondesson on generalized gamma convolutions; Biane, Pitman and Yor (2001); Andrews and Mallows (1974); Kennedy and Pendleton (1985); Swendsen and Wang (1987); Edwards and Sokal (1988); Besag (1974, 1975); Jerrum and Sinclair (1993); Newman (1976).
