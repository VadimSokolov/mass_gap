This appendix collects proofs and statements of classical results used in the text in the forms the book needs, followed by three longer arguments that the text used in outline: the equation for the mixed partial, lowest order perturbation theory for a Wilson loop, and convexity and curvature for the sliced link (the last stated to be incomplete). The classical items are Bernstein's theorem on completely monotone functions, Stieltjes inversion, Laplace asymptotics, log convexity of Laplace transforms, the Goldie and Steutel theorem, Amos's inequalities for Bessel ratios, the SU(2) heat kernel image sum, the mean of the Biane Pitman Yor variable and Laguerre's theorem for the theta product.

## From the literature

Bernstein's theorem on completely monotone functions (Bernstein 1929); Stieltjes inversion; Laplace's method; the Goldie and Steutel theorem; Amos's inequalities for ratios of modified Bessel functions (Amos 1974); the mean of the Biane Pitman Yor variable (Biane, Pitman and Yor 2001); Laguerre's theorem for the theta product.
