This appendix gives worked solutions to selected exercises of the chapters, organised by chapter. The solutions are short derivations and computations, for example the variance of a sample mean for a stationary chain with autocorrelations, and they follow the methods developed in the chapters on Markov chain Monte Carlo, lattice gauge theory and the Migdal Kadanoff ladder.
