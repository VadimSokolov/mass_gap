This appendix fixes the notation and conventions of the book, gives a glossary of its technical terms, and a dictionary between statistics and physics. The dictionary translates models, structure and computation between the two vocabularies: Gibbs measures and Markov random fields, partition function and normalising constant, heat bath and Gibbs sampler, pseudolikelihood, data augmentation and latent variables, renormalization group and block averaging, and the terms of this book such as the ladder, the count and the Thorin measure. It is meant for statisticians reading physics and physicists reading statistics.
