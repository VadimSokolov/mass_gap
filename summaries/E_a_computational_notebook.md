This appendix lists the scripts of the book in the order of the argument, with what each computes, the result to expect, and the approximate running time. The sections follow the book: positivity and the two routes, two dimensions and Monte Carlo methods, the ladder, the heat kernel, four dimensions, and contraction, counts, zeros and Bayesian computation, followed by conventions. It is meant for a reader who wants to reproduce or extend the computations, and complements the index of programs in Appendix A.
